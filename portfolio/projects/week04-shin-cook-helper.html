🍅 토마토 (Tomato Pasta)

Tomatoes are an essential ingredient in many traditional Italian dishes.

Using fresh tomatoes makes any recipe incredibly delicious!

📋 Cooking Steps

First, boil the pasta in hot water.

Then, chop the fresh tomatoes.

Next, fry garlic in a pan with olive oil.

After that, add the tomatoes to make the sauce.

Finally, mix them all together and serve.

👨‍🍳 "I have boiled pasta many times before.

Yesterday, I fried some cherry tomatoes for my friends."

🍣 연어 (Salmon Steak)

Are you feeling adventurous today?

Try this amazing salmon dish as a luxurious appetizer!

📋 Cooking Steps

First, season the salmon with salt and pepper.

Then, heat up your pan.

Next, you can bake it in the oven or grill it on the pan.

After that, you can also steam some vegetables for a side dish.

Finally, enjoy your healthy meal!

👨‍🍳 "I have grilled salmon often at parties.

Last week, I baked a large piece of salmon for dinner."

🥔 감자 (French Fries)

This is a traditional and delicious dish that everyone loves.

Potatoes are the perfect ingredient for a simple yet tasty appetizer.

📋 Cooking Steps

First, wash the potatoes cleanly.

Then, you can boil them if you want mashed potatoes.

Next, cut them into long strips.

After that, fry them in hot oil until crispy.

Finally, put some salt and eat.

👨‍🍳 "I have fried potatoes for my kids countless times.

Yesterday, I boiled them for a quick and healthy snack."

🍗 닭고기 (Roasted Chicken)

Chicken is perhaps the most popular ingredient around the world.

If you want a fantastic meal, this recipe will never disappoint you!

📋 Cooking Steps

First, mix the chicken with your favorite spices.

Then, preheat your oven.

Next, bake or roast the chicken in the oven.

After that, grill it slightly for a crispy skin.

Finally, serve the meal to your family.

👨‍🍳 "I have roasted chicken for Thanksgiving many times.

Just yesterday, I grilled a chicken breast for a light salad."
